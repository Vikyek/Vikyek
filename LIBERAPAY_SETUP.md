# Liberapay Profile Setup & Activation Guide

This document contains the ready-to-paste bilingual profile copy and step-by-step activation instructions for **[liberapay.com/Vikyek](https://liberapay.com/Vikyek)**.

## 1. Step-by-Step Activation Instructions

1. **Sign Up / Log In**:
   - Navigate to [https://liberapay.com/](https://liberapay.com/).
   - Click **Log in / Sign up** and select **Log in with GitHub**.
   - Authorize with your GitHub account `Vikyek`.
2. **Confirm Username**:
   - Verify that your Liberapay username is `Vikyek` (url: `https://liberapay.com/Vikyek`).
3. **Configure Profile Description**:
   - Go to [https://liberapay.com/Vikyek/edit](https://liberapay.com/Vikyek/edit).
   - Under **Statement** / **Pitch**, paste the tagline below.
   - Under **Biography** / **Description**, paste the English version under the English tab and the Polish version under the Polish (*Polski*) tab.
4. **Set Living Essentials Goal**:
   - In profile settings, set your recurring goal:
     - **Goal Amount**: `2,710 PLN / month` (or `~625 EUR / month` / `~145 EUR / week`).
     - **Goal Description**: `Monthly Living Essentials (Studio Rent & Utilities) for Full-Time Open-Source Systems Engineering`.
5. **Connect Payout Processor**:
   - Open **Payouts / Bank Account**:
   - Connect **Stripe** (Polish bank account payout in PLN) or **PayPal**.
   - Note: Liberapay charges 0% platform commission; payment processing fees are passed directly to Stripe/PayPal.
